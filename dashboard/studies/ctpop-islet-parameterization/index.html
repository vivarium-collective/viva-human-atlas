🔬 Findings — what this study taught us

◆ F-01 other novel literature
Binding the documented healthy islet composition (54% beta) to Topp2000's initial beta-cell mass reproduces the model's own default (b_ref=37) and the model settles at its physiological fixed point (glucose ~100 mg/dL over 4,000 model-days). Binding the documented T2D-relevant beta-depleted composition (30% beta) scales the initial mass down to ~20.56 and the identical model crosses Topp2000's beta-cell-mass bifurcation into its pathological fixed point (glucose ~600 mg/dL, beta-cell mass and insulin collapsing toward 0) -- the CTpop composition mechanistically shifts which of the model's two stable regimes the glucose trajectory lands in, not merely its numeric value.

Limitations

What this study explicitly does NOT validate (prevents overclaim).

  • `ISLET_COMPOSITION` is documented literature/HRA-ASCT+B composition (Cabrera et al. 2006 PNAS; Brissova et al. 2005), not a live HRA CTpop API pull for a specific RUI-registered tissue block -- a live CTpop endpoint is not (as of this writing) publicly exposed as JSON.
  • Binding is initial-mass only: only the beta-cell fraction enters the Topp2000 model, and only as the initial condition for B, not as a rate/kinetic parameter -- alpha and delta fractions are identified (`ISLET_COMPOSITION`) but not bound to any Topp2000 parameter (the model has no glucagon/somatostatin compartment to bind them to).
  • The 30% beta-depleted scenario is a single illustrative T2D-relevant composition point, not a distribution over real measured islets -- the exact glucose/insulin/beta-mass endpoints (600 / ~0 / ~0) are specific to this one fraction and this one model's parameterization, not a general dose-response curve.

Discovery implications

Where this study's results leave the mechanism model — and what to investigate next.

✓ Resolved uncertainties

  • CTpop islet composition, bound as an initial-condition parameter (beta-cell mass), does mechanistically change Topp2000's predicted glucose regulation -- realizing (for one covered FTU model and one composition-to-parameter binding) the RUI -> CTpop -> model parameterization vision `ftu-model-coverage`'s `ctpop_parameter_stub` only stubbed.
  • The composition shift from healthy (54% beta) to T2D-relevant beta-depleted (30% beta) composition is large enough, in this model's parameterization, to cross a real bifurcation between the model's physiological and pathological fixed points -- not just perturb a number within one regime.

● Remaining uncertainties

  • Does a LIVE HRA CTpop count (for a specific RUI-registered islet tissue block) differ materially from the documented literature-average composition used here, and if so, in which direction?
  • Should alpha/delta cell fractions bind to separate Topp2000-family or extended-model parameters (e.g. a glucagon-secretion term), rather than folding all islet composition information into the single beta-cell-mass initial condition?
