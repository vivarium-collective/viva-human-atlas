Question & approach

Does binding HRA CTpop islet cell-type composition to the Topp2000 beta-cell-mass model (BIOMD0000000341) change the predicted glucose regulation -- realizing the RUI -> CTpop -> model parameterization vision Katy Boerner / Peter Hunter / SPARC describe, for the pancreatic islet FTU `ftu-model-coverage` already found covered?
Summary. `ftu-model-coverage` identified the pancreatic islet as an HRA functional tissue unit already covered by curated BioModels (Topp2000, Jiang2007, Bertram2007) and stubbed (via `ctpop_parameter_stub`) the islet's beta/alpha/delta cell types (CL:0000169/0171/0173) as candidate CTpop (Cell Type Populations, Bueckle 2026) parameterization targets, without binding any real count. This study takes that next step for Topp2000 (BIOMD0000000341, Topp et al. 2000 J Theor Biol): it binds a documented HRA-aligned islet beta-cell-fraction (a live CTpop API pull is not publicly exposed as JSON as of this writing, so this study uses documented literature/HRA-ASCT+B composition values instead, cited in `ISLET_COMPOSITION` -- beta 54%, alpha 34%, delta 10%, PP/epsilon ~2%, per Cabrera et al. 2006 PNAS and Brissova et al. 2005) onto the Topp model's initial beta-cell mass B, via `B_init = b_ref * (beta_fraction / 0.54)` where `b_ref` (37, the model's own SBML-declared default initial B) is read out of the model, not hardcoded. The Topp model is deliberately built to operate on a DAYS timescale (beta-cell-mass turnover is slow relative to glucose/insulin kinetics), so this study runs it out to 4,000 model-time-units (~11 years) -- long enough for the model's slow beta-cell-mass compartment to reach a new steady state. Running both a healthy islet (54% beta, b_init=37, reproducing the model's own default) and a beta-depleted islet (30% beta, the T2D-relevant beta-cell loss, b_init~20.6) shows the composition binding lands on a real mechanistic knife-edge in this model: the healthy islet settles at the model's physiological fixed point (glucose ~100 mg/dL, a normal fasting-range value), while the beta-depleted islet crosses Topp2000's bifurcation into the pathological fixed point (glucose ~600 mg/dL, severe hyperglycemia, with beta-cell mass and insulin both collapsing toward zero) -- exactly the "mild vs. extreme beta-cell loss" bifurcation behavior the original Topp et al. 2000 paper describes as its central diabetes-progression finding. So binding CTpop composition to this model doesn't just nudge a number, it flips which of the model's two stable regimes the trajectory lands in.

🔬 Findings — what this study taught us

◆ Binding the documented healthy islet composition (54% beta) to Topp2000's initial beta-cell mass reproduces the model's own default (b_ref=37) and the model settles at its physiological fixed point (glucose ~100 mg/dL over 4,000 model-days). Binding the documented T2D-relevant beta-depleted composition (30% beta) scales the initial mass down to ~20.56 and the identical model crosses Topp2000's beta-cell-mass bifurcation into its pathological fixed point (glucose ~600 mg/dL, beta-cell mass and insulin collapsing toward 0) -- the CTpop composition mechanistically shifts which of the model's two stable regimes the glucose trajectory lands in, not merely its numeric value.

Limitations

What this study explicitly does NOT validate (prevents overclaim).

  • `ISLET_COMPOSITION` is documented literature/HRA-ASCT+B composition (Cabrera et al. 2006 PNAS; Brissova et al. 2005), not a live HRA CTpop API pull for a specific RUI-registered tissue block -- a live CTpop endpoint is not (as of this writing) publicly exposed as JSON.
  • Binding is initial-mass only: only the beta-cell fraction enters the Topp2000 model, and only as the initial condition for B, not as a rate/kinetic parameter -- alpha and delta fractions are identified (`ISLET_COMPOSITION`) but not bound to any Topp2000 parameter (the model has no glucagon/somatostatin compartment to bind them to).
  • The 30% beta-depleted scenario is a single illustrative T2D-relevant composition point, not a distribution over real measured islets -- the exact glucose/insulin/beta-mass endpoints (600 / ~0 / ~0) are specific to this one fraction and this one model's parameterization, not a general dose-response curve.
